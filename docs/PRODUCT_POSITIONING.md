# Product positioning

_Last updated: 2026-08-03_

## The one-line positioning statement

> SkillSplore is a moderated learning noticeboard that helps learners and people with useful
> knowledge or skills find each other.

Use this statement (or a close paraphrase) wherever the product needs a short public description —
metadata, About page, pitch decks, support replies. Do not describe SkillSplore as a "marketplace"
in anything a visitor, learner or provider can see. "Marketplace" may still be used in internal
engineering docs, code comments and this docs/ directory, where it accurately describes the
underlying two-sided structure.

## Who it serves

SkillSplore is not limited to academic tutoring. It serves anyone who wants to learn something and
anyone who can teach something, across:

- Academic subjects (maths, sciences, languages, humanities, exam prep)
- Music (instruments, singing, production, theory)
- Art and design (drawing, painting, digital art, photography)
- Technology (programming, data, CAD/engineering tools)
- Business and professional skills (Excel, public speaking, CV writing)
- Practical and lifestyle skills (cooking, sport and fitness coaching)

Not every provider is a "tutor" — a saxophone teacher, a personal trainer and a maths tutor are all
providers on the same platform, described in whatever way fits their subject or skill. See
[`TERMINOLOGY.md`](TERMINOLOGY.md) for exact word choices.

## How search and requests work

Two equally-visible paths, both from the homepage:

1. **Search directly** — browse or filter people by subject/skill, location, format, rate,
   experience and availability.
2. **Post a request** — describe what you want to learn; suitable people can respond. Requests are
   moderated before providers are notified.

Both paths lead to direct messaging between the two people once they choose to talk; SkillSplore
does not process payments, supervise sessions, or guarantee an outcome (see
[`ARRANGEMENT_DISCLAIMER`](../apps/web/src/lib/pricingCopy.ts)).

## What is moderated, and what isn't

**Moderated:** profiles (reviewed before becoming public), requests (reviewed before providers are
notified), reviews (require a genuine completed engagement), reports (profiles, requests, messages,
reviews, users can all be reported), and verification labels (each states exactly what was checked,
by whom, and when — never a generic "Verified" badge).

**Not guaranteed:** that any provider will respond, that a match will occur, the quality or outcome
of any session, or the identity/qualifications of a provider beyond what a specific verification
label states. SkillSplore is not a party to any agreement between a learner and a provider, and does
not employ, insure or supervise anyone listed on the platform.

## Claims that require evidence before they can be published

Do not publish, re-add, or imply any of the following without a real, current, verifiable basis:

- Specific tutor/user/subject counts as a trust signal (e.g. "90+ subjects", "500+ tutors")
- "0% fees", "no fees", "free forever", or any other permanent pricing promise
- "Verified tutors" without a defined, disclosed verification process behind the label
- "Guaranteed responses", "guaranteed students", or similar outcome guarantees
- "Safe and secure" as a blanket claim (state specific, precise safety mechanisms instead)
- "Best tutors" / "trusted experts" or other unearned superlatives
- Testimonials, reviews or activity that did not really happen

## Product stage

SkillSplore is **pre-launch**. Public copy should say things like "Launching soon", "Preparing for
launch", "Join the early community" — honest about being early without looking abandoned. Do not
imply an established, large platform. See [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) for what pre-launch means in
practice.
